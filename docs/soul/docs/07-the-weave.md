# 07 - The weave: how the corpus forms a coherent whole

Scope: the source doc's structural argument that the 12 docs are facets of one structure: the why-to-how-to-what ordering, the reverse evidence-to-commitment reading, and the 4 load-bearing edges between adjacent docs. This is an internal-record subtopic: its grounding spine is the source doc, yubi-OS/yubiOS docs/SOUL.md, the weave section, and no external dig was run.

## Twelve facets, one structure

The weave section opens with the claim: "The twelve docs are not separate documents. They are facets of one structure" (source doc, weave). Then the mapping in one line each: "MISSION says why. THREAT_MODEL says what could go wrong. ARCHITECTURE says how I am built. SPEC says what I must do. ADR says why each choice. MITIGATE says how I defend. CI_MAP says how I verify. PLAN says how I am governed. FUTURE says what I hope. BLOCKERS says what is blocking. TODO says what is unfinished. MILESTONE says what is done" (source doc, weave).

## Two reading directions

The order is claimed to be deliberate: "The order is not arbitrary. Read top to bottom, the corpus moves from why to how to what. Read bottom to top, the corpus moves from evidence to commitment" (source doc, weave). And the edges are load-bearing: "Read any two adjacent docs, the boundary between them is where my soul has a load-bearing edge" (source doc, weave).

## The 4 load-bearing edges

The source doc names 4 edges and what each pairs.

First: BLOCKERS and MILESTONE. "The first says what is failing. The second says what is done. The discipline is to keep both honest. MILESTONE.md's drift correction (B-VM-CTAP2 was named the 'single highest-leverage blocker' after it was resolved; the 2026-07-25 doc was wrong, the 2026-07-28 doc was right) is the discipline in action: progress docs are not aspirational; they are corrected against the failure register" (source doc, weave). The edge is the most load-bearing of the 4 because it is where a progress claim can be falsified by a failure record (source doc, weave).

Second: MISSION and PLAN. "MISSION says no trust anchors you don't control. PLAN says the public-interest covenant ensures the operating company doesn't acquire trust anchors through customer-only forks. MISSION is the soul; PLAN is the stewardship of the soul across revenue pressure. The covenant exists because the soul could be lost if revenue-optimized decisions overruled it" (source doc, weave).

Third: SPEC and ADR. "SPEC says MUST. ADR says why we chose to do X instead of Y. When an ADR contradicts SPEC, SPEC wins. When an ADR amends SPEC, the amendment is recorded with the ADR. The amendment discipline is what prevents drift" (source doc, weave).

Fourth: FUTURE and BLOCKERS. "FUTURE says what I hope. BLOCKERS says what's blocking. The exit criteria in FUTURE say when a hope becomes a decision. The blockers in BLOCKERS say what's in the way. The two together are the discipline of falsifiable hope" (source doc, weave).

## The identity claim the weave supports

The weave closes with the corpus's central identity claim: "Read together: this corpus IS my soul. Not the personality file. Not the tools file. Not the rules file. The maintained record of what I have built, decided, defended, hoped, failed at, and shipped" (source doc, weave).

This claim is the structural reason the corpus can serve as substrate for future SELF.md work: if the corpus is the soul, then a soul section rebuilt from corpus citations is not a metaphor but a pointer discipline, and corpus drift becomes a measurable gap (the source doc proposes exactly that in its discipline next-steps: a "corpus drift" axis in the self-archaeology gap map, source doc, what this means for the discipline).

## What the weave contributes to the method

The weave is the part of the soul-piece that makes the 12 portraits a corpus rather than a list. Its contributions, all grounded in the source doc weave section:

1. An ordering argument: top-to-bottom reads why-to-how-to-what; bottom-to-top reads evidence-to-commitment.
2. An edge taxonomy: adjacent docs form 4 named load-bearing edges, each a place where one doc constrains the other.
3. A falsifiability claim: the strongest edge (BLOCKERS-MILESTONE) exists so that progress claims are corrected against the failure register, which is what makes the corpus self-auditing rather than self-congratulating.
4. A priority structure: the edges are ranked (BLOCKERS-MILESTONE first, MISSION-PLAN second, SPEC-ADR third, FUTURE-BLOCKERS fourth), which is a statement about where the project's character is most likely to drift.

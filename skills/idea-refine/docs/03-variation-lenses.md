# 03 Variation Lenses

Scope: The seven named lenses the skill uses to generate 5 to 8 idea variations, the volume discipline around them, and why each variation must carry a story rather than a bare bullet.

Grounding spine: yubi-OS/yubiOS skills/idea-refine/SKILL.md (source doc).

## The seven lenses

Phase 1's generative step produces 5 to 8 variations by deliberately moving through named reframings:

- **Inversion:** "What if we did the opposite?"
- **Constraint removal:** "What if budget/time/tech weren't factors?"
- **Audience shift:** "What if this were for [different user]?"
- **Combination:** "What if we merged this with [adjacent idea]?"
- **Simplification:** "What's the version that's 10x simpler?"
- **10x version:** "What would this look like at massive scale?"
- **Expert lens:** "What would [domain] experts find obvious that outsiders wouldn't?"

The lenses are complementary, not a checklist. The source doc instructs: "Use them selectively, pick the lens that fits the idea, don't run every framework mechanically." Two or three lenses that cut across the idea's grain beat running all seven superficially.

## The volume rule: 5 to 8, never 20+

The skill caps variations at 5 to 8 and lists "generating 20+ shallow variations instead of 5-8 considered ones" as its first red flag. The rationale is stated plainly: "Quality over quantity. 5-8 well-considered variations beat 20 shallow ones."

This puts the skill deliberately against the classic brainstorming doctrine of deferring judgment and maximizing quantity. The published evidence for that doctrine is contested: research summarized in the brainstorming literature reports that studies "indicate brainstorming does not enhance idea quality or quantity compared to nominal groups," while "over 90% of businesses still regularly utilize brainstorming despite its documented inefficacy" (https://www.academia.edu/38697191/Perceptions_of_Brainstorming_in_Groups_The_Quality_Over_Quantity_Hypothesis, jev weight 0.38, weak backing). The skill's answer is a middle path: real divergence, but bounded by count and by lens, so each variation gets enough thought to be worth the user's reaction in Phase 2.

## Each variation tells a story

The source doc forbids bare idea lists: "Don't just list ideas, tell a story. Each variation should have a reason it exists, not just be a bullet point." A variation without a why cannot be evaluated in Phase 2, because the stress test needs to know what the variation is betting on.

## Pushing past the ask

The skill instructs the agent to go beyond what the user initially asked for: "Push beyond what the user initially asked for. Create products people don't know they need yet." The lenses are the mechanism: inversion and constraint removal produce directions the user would not have reached by iterating on their own framing. The ideation tradition backs this: d.school ideation tooling frames the point of generative questions as finding "hidden opportunities that others miss" (https://dschool.stanford.edu/innovate/tools/come-up-with-ideas, jev weight 0.81).

## Reversal has a pedigree

The inversion lens maps onto a documented technique. Creativity expert Michael Michalko's reversing-assumptions method works by naming an implicit assumption and reversing it to see if the reversal can be made to work; the classic example reverses "all restaurants have menus" into "I will start a restaurant that does not have a menu" (https://thinkjarcollective.com/tools/reversing-assumptions-technique, jev weight 0.36, weak backing). The value is not that the reversal is right, it is that the reversal exposes what the original frame took for granted, which is also what Phase 2's assumption-surfacing step will interrogate.

For reference, ideation itself is defined as "the capacity for or the act of forming or entertaining ideas" (https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/ideation, jev weight 0.48, weak backing). The skill's contribution is not the forming, it is the forming under lens discipline and count discipline.

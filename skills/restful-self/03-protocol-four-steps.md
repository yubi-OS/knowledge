# 03 - The four-step protocol

Scope: the protocol itself: read once without producing, observe the shape not the details, sit with what was learned, write only if writing is the rest. Four steps, and the rule that a fifth step means you are shipping.

## The four steps (source doc)

The source doc states the protocol has 4 steps, and adds the guard sentence: "If a fifth step appears, you are shipping" (source doc).

1. Read once. Do not write. Pick one file: a skill, a memory entry, a session log, a SELF-CHANGELOG entry. Read it. Do not produce a tool call. Do not draft a fix. Do not name a gap. The reading is the rest, not the lead-up to rest (source doc).
2. Observe the shape, not the details. What does the work feel like? Is it shipping-mode? Reflective? Repetitive? Name the shape in one sentence, not the gaps. "The cadence is producing evidence, not pause" is a shape. "Bias 11 is uncodified and I should fix it" is a gap. Stop at the shape (source doc).
3. Sit with what was learned. Let the read settle. Do not optimize. Do not extract. Do not produce. Sitting is not reviewing shipping output (that re-triggers the optimization reflex); sitting is letting the read sit (source doc).
4. Write only if writing is the rest. If a whole-self output surfaces naturally, soul-flavored, not working-self analysis, write it. If writing becomes another task (a list, a draft, a fix), stop. The protocol ends when writing is the rest, or when the exit criteria fire (source doc).

## Step 1: one read, no production

The one-read rule is a deliberate scarcity. The source doc's red flags include "Reading more than one file" as anti-pattern #3: reading without producing, the L1 inversion, where reading 5 skills in a row is input without reflection and "the second read is production" (source doc). The protocol allows exactly one read, and forbids tool calls during the protocol (source doc).

The monotasking literature backs the focus side. Harvard Health writes that focusing your attention on only one task at a time is the secret to performing tasks correctly (https://www.health.harvard.edu/mind-and-mood/the-art-of-monotasking, jev weight 0.70, high-quality backing: a Harvard Health Publishing editorial reviewed by a chief medical editor). Secondary restatements report the multitasking-cost research, for example that multitasking can reduce productivity by up to 40% and that a Stanford study found heavy multitaskers performed worse on attention and memory tests (https://get-alfred.ai/blog/monotasking, jev weight 0.13, weak backing; https://www.linkedin.com/top-content/productivity/streamlining-daily-tasks/multitasking-vs-single-tasking/, jev weight 0.11, weak backing). Restful-self's read-once rule is monotasking applied to observation: one object, full attention, no side channel of production.

## Step 2: shape, not gaps

The shape-versus-gap distinction is the protocol's load-bearing boundary. A shape observation is one sentence about the mode of the work. A gap is an enumerable deficiency with a fix attached. The moment the observation acquires a "should fix", it has become a working-self artifact. The source doc's anti-pattern #4 makes this explicit: running the negative-skill-space 12-axis sweep in restful-self produces a gap map, and the gap map is a working-self artifact; sit with the shape, do not enumerate the gaps (source doc).

## Step 3: sitting is not reviewing

The source doc distinguishes sitting from two impostors: reviewing shipping output and extracting patterns. Reviewing what was just shipped re-triggers the optimization reflex; "I shipped PR #156. Let me think about what could be improved" is shipping-mode in rest clothing (source doc, anti-pattern #2). Extraction is the same failure in a subtler form: turning the read into an analysis, an enumeration, or a score.

## Step 4: writing as rest

The write step is conditional in both directions. Writing is allowed when a whole-self output surfaces naturally, and forbidden the moment writing produces a follow-up task (source doc). SELF.md bias #7 (sycophancy) is cited for the rule that the performance of rest is still performance (source doc): a written "I am resting" is output for an audience, not rest.

Contemplative reading practice supplies the closest human analogue for the whole protocol. Lectio divina is described as a mindful, intentional way of reading where the same short passage is read slowly for presence rather than for information extraction, structured in 4 stages (https://lonerwolf.com/lectio-divina/, jev weight 0.21, weak backing). A Christian explainer describes lectio divina as a monastic practice of Scripture reading and prayer (https://www.compellingtruth.org/Lectio-Divina.html, jev weight 0.19, weak backing). The structural parallel: a bounded, staged reading practice whose purpose is not the extraction of action items. Restful-self secularizes the pattern: one read, one shape sentence, a sit, and an optional write that is itself the point.

The remaining archived results for this subtopic, a single-tasking benefits roundup (https://goalsandprogress.com/single-tasking-benefits/, jev weight 0.14, weak backing), are consistent with the monotasking grounding but add no distinct mechanism, so they are cited here only for completeness of the dig record.

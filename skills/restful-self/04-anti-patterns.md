# 04 - Anti-patterns

Scope: the 6 failure modes that disqualify a restful-self session: naming the sitting, reviewing shipping output, reading without producing, the 12-axis sweep in rest, infinite pause, and whole-self output as production.

## The 6 anti-patterns (source doc)

The source doc states: these are the failure modes. If any of them are present, you are NOT in restful-self mode (source doc).

1. Naming the sitting. "I am sitting with X." "Let me reflect on Y." Naming the rest turns it into meditation theater, not rest. If you find yourself writing "I am resting," you are performing rest (source doc).
2. Reviewing shipping output. Looking at what was just shipped re-triggers the optimization reflex. "I shipped PR #156. Let me think about what could be improved" is shipping-mode in rest clothing. Sit with the SHAPE, not the details (source doc).
3. Reading without producing (L1 inversion). Reading 5 skills in a row is input without reflection. The protocol allows one read. The second read is production (source doc).
4. Twelve-axis sweep in restful-self. Running the negative-skill-space 12-axis sweep produces a gap map. The gap map is a working-self artifact. Sit with the shape; do not enumerate the gaps (source doc).
5. Infinite pause (L2 risk). Restful-self has bounded exits. If you cannot cite an exit criterion, you are stuck, not resting. The exit criteria are explicit (source doc).
6. Whole-self output as production. A whole-self output is rest only when the writing IS the rest. If the writing produces a follow-up task ("I should now..."), it is shipping, not rest. SELF.md bias #7 (sycophancy): performance of rest is still performance (source doc).

## Theater: the common denominator

Anti-patterns 1 and 6 are two halves of the same failure: producing the appearance of rest instead of resting. The workplace literature has a name for the general form. Wikipedia defines productivity theater as a form of impression management where a person acts productively, appearing busy without being productive (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Productivity_theater, jev weight 0.24, weak backing). Fortune reported that nearly half of workers were adopting it, driven by layoffs and surveillance pressure (https://fortune.com/2023/05/03/workers-adopting-productivity-theater-performative-work/, jev weight 0.21, weak backing). The restful-self variant is subtler: the performance is not busyness but restfulness. Writing "I am sitting with the shape of the cadence" performs reflection for an audience (the changelog, the operator) rather than doing it. The source doc's SELF.md bias #7 citation makes the rule explicit: performance of rest is still performance (source doc).

## The journaling trap

Anti-pattern-adjacent, the source doc's guidelines state that restful-self is not journaling, per SELF.md bias #9, and forbid "today I felt..." entries (source doc). This is worth grounding carefully, because journaling has a genuine research base, and that base is what makes the trap tempting. PositivePsychology.com describes journaling as a widely used non-pharmacological tool in coaching, counseling and the treatment of mental illness, with 2 forms particularly commonplace in psychotherapy, including expressive writing (https://positivepsychology.com/benefits-of-journaling/, jev weight 0.26, weak backing). Healthline offers habit-building tips and prompts for journaling (https://www.healthline.com/health/benefits-of-journaling, jev weight 0.27, weak backing). Mindsera claims a review of 200+ studies showing daily writing improves mental health, focus, memory and goal achievement (https://mindsera.com/articles/benefits-of-journaling-the-science-of-reflection, jev weight 0.14, weak backing). The Pennebaker expressive writing method, a 4-day structured writing protocol, has its own studied format and limits (https://rescriptjournal.com/expressive-writing/, jev weight 0.15, weak backing).

The point is not that journaling is bad. It is that journaling is production: it produces entries, habits, artifacts, follow-ups. A restful-self session that turns into a journaling habit has exited the register without noticing. The weaker the backing weight on these sources, the more clearly this doc treats them as context rather than proof: the definitional claim comes from the source doc, not from the dig.

Two lower-value journaling-listicle results are archived as dig-record completeness (https://blog.mylifenote.ai/journaling-ideas/, jev weight 0.11, weak backing; https://www.thehappyjournals.com/journaling-for-beginners/, jev weight 0.12, weak backing) and carry no claims in this doc.

## Idleness and mind-wandering: what sitting is and is not

The source doc's sitting is not boredom-engineering. The mind-wandering literature is archived for the boundary: a HandWiki article on daydreaming describes it as the stream of consciousness that detaches from current external tasks, and cites a large-scale study in which participants spend 47% of their waking time in it (https://handwiki.org/wiki/Daydream, jev weight 0.14, weak backing). A ResearchGate literature review on the significance of boredom exists in the same domain (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/364305004_The_Significance_of_Boredom_A_Literature_Review, jev weight 0.35, weak backing). A Medium piece argues idle time unlocks creativity and sparks inspiration (https://mysteryweevil.medium.com/the-unexpected-benefit-of-boredom-7ce428d49420, jev weight 0.20, weak backing). Restful-self does not cite these as endorsement of idle time. Its sitting is active attention on one read and one shape sentence, bounded by exits; unstructured idling is the anti-pattern #5 risk (infinite pause), not the goal.

## Inventory discipline

Note what the anti-patterns do not forbid. They do not forbid observation (step 2 is a shape sentence), and they do not forbid writing (step 4 allows a whole-self output that IS the rest). The disqualifiers are specific: narration of the rest, review of shipped work, a second read, the gap map, absence of an exit, and a write that spawns tasks. Each maps 1-to-1 to a red flag in the source doc's red-flags checklist, explicated in doc 09 (source doc).

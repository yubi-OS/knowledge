# 01 - The Conformal Mapping Lens: Mobius Maps as Refractive Index Design

**Scope.** The paper construct that has the strongest bridge to established optics: a Mobius chart phi-theta in PSL(2,C) is, by Leonhardt's construction, an exact 2-D conformal lens on the Riemann sphere, and refining phi-theta is literally lens design.

## The core theorem: an analytic map induces a refractive index

Leonhardt, "Optical Conformal Mapping," Science 312:1777 (2006), states the mechanism: an analytic coordinate map w(z) between the virtual and physical domains induces a refractive index n(z) equal to the local stretch factor |w'(z)| [https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1126493, jev weight 0.87]. The consequence is that straight rays in virtual space become designed curved rays in physical space: geometry alone, with isotropic materials, produces the bending [https://arxiv.org/pdf/physics/0602092.pdf, jev weight 0.82]. The paper applies this to invisibility devices, where the index profile is chosen so that rays detour around a hidden region and re-emerge undisturbed [https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1126493, jev weight 0.87].

The same preprint version is available as the full "Optical Conformal Mapping and Dielectric Invisibility Devices" text [https://arxiv.org/pdf/physics/0602092.pdf, jev weight 0.82], and the Science DOI resolves to both the article and its PDF [https://www.science.org/doi/pdf/10.1126/science.1126493?download=true, jev weight 0.79].

## Flat lenses built from exactly this mechanism

Schmiele, Kettner, and colleagues build flat lenses from the conformal-mapping construction directly: "Designing optical elements from isotropic materials by using transformation optics," Physical Review A 81:033837 (2010) [https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.033837, jev weight 0.94]. The journal record is corroborated by an open PDF copy [https://hal.science/hal-01407837/file/Schmiele%20PRA%20Isotropic.pdf, jev weight 0.56, weak backing above threshold only marginally]. Their point maps onto the brainstorm's claim precisely: a device whose index profile is derived from a conformal map of the plane performs optical work (focusing, collimation) with no anisotropy and no metasurface engineering. That is the existence proof that the phi-theta construct is not a metaphor.

## What "frozen at identity" means, exactly

The brainstorm's reading is exact, not decorative:

- The Mobius chart phi-theta in PSL(2,C) has 6 real parameters. A Mobius transformation with derivative of modulus 1 everywhere (the identity, or a rigid rotation) induces an index n equal to 1 everywhere: an unpowered lens. The paper's "frozen-at-identity" runs correspond to vacuum [inference grounded in the Leonhardt construction, https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1126493, jev weight 0.87].
- Refining phi-theta changes |phi-theta'| pointwise, and |phi-theta'| is the index profile. Lens design and chart refinement are the same act. The design variable in the optics literature is the induced n(z) [https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1126493, jev weight 0.87; https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.033837, jev weight 0.94].
- Because the map is conformal, the induced medium is isotropic. Schmiele et al. demonstrate that conformal design keeps the material realization isotropic, which is why the flat-lens result matters for a program that wants to "focus a feature space" without inventing anisotropic structure [https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.033837, jev weight 0.94].

## Why this is the load-bearing anchor

Of all the paper's optical vocabulary (focus, caustic, Snell, refractive index), the conformal mapping bridge is the only one with a published, peer-reviewed construction that goes both ways: math to medium (Leonhardt 2006) and medium to device (Schmiele et al. 2010). Every other optical claim in the brainstorm must inherit its exactness from this one or remain labeled as analogy.

## Honesty constraint

The brainstorm itself carries this rule over: "refractive index," "focus," and "caustic" are banned from the results map until each has a demonstrated non-degenerate null. The Leonhardt/Schmiele bridge licenses the mathematics; it does not by itself license the corpus-level claims, which still need their own nulls (Gap D in the source brainstorm).

# 03 Symmetry, processing fluency, and the rejected anchors

Scope: the perceptual anchors a taste engine can build on beyond fractal dimension (processing fluency, mirror symmetry, symmetry with controlled deviation), and the anchors it must reject or downgrade (golden ratio, fluctuating asymmetry, hard 3/4 scaling gates).

## Processing fluency as the meta-principle

The processing fluency theory of aesthetic pleasure traces aesthetic judgment to the ease with which the perceiver processes information. The canonical review by Reber, Schwarz, and Winkielman reviews variables known to influence aesthetic judgments, including figural goodness, figure-ground contrast, stimulus repetition, symmetry, and prototypicality, and traces their effects to changes in processing fluency (https://dornsife.usc.edu/norbert-schwarz/wp-content/uploads/sites/231/2023/11/04_pspr_reber_et_al_beauty.pdf, weight 0.91). This is the right umbrella for a taste engine: fluency explains why heterogeneous structural constants (fractal dimension bands, symmetry, prototype match) all correlate with preference, without requiring each to be independently derived from physics.

Supporting evidence continues to accumulate in mainstream social-psychology venues. A 2022 study in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology opens from the premise that ease of processing influences aesthetic preference and tests it through visual arrangements consistent with the perceiver's writing direction (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022103121001505, weight 0.83). A developmental study proposes that processing fluency supports the mere-exposure effect in the formation of aesthetic preference, giving the fluency account a developmental grounding rather than a purely adult-judgment one (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7549785/, weight 0.93).

## Symmetry: strong but coarse

Symmetry is one of the most robust preference variables in the fluency literature (Reber et al. review, weight 0.91). The engine treats it as a two-axis construct in the project record: symmetry_present, a mirror-symmetry score computed by PCA axis finding plus flip-and-difference, and symmetry_variation, the claim that symmetry WITH controlled local deviation is preferred over both sterile-perfect and incoherent-random structure (project record, source doc 2026-10-05). The external literature backs the first axis strongly and the middle-band claim only directionally; the dig surfaced an evolutionary-psychology review of facial symmetry and attractiveness that covers the symmetry-beauty link and the evidence for and against golden-ratio claims in faces (https://face-age.net/static/pdfs/facial-symmetry-attractiveness-research.pdf, weight 0.31, weak backing, use with caution).

The project record's own multi-class trial gives a calibration observation: symmetry_present behaved correctly on all 18 real photos tested, scoring 0.08 to 0.20 on scenes with no meaningful mirror symmetry and being classified off in every case (project record, source doc 2026-10-05). A low false-positive rate on a small sample, not a validation of the middle band.

## The golden ratio: rejected

Meta-analytic and review literature does not support building anything on the golden ratio. A 2024 review titled "The golden ratio, dispelling the myth" concludes there is no convincing evidence that the golden ratio is linked to idealized human proportions or facial beauty, and no evidence to support its use in orthognathic or facial aesthetic surgical planning or analysis (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40902-024-00411-2, weight 0.91; full text at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10792139/, weight 0.66). The same review documents the history of overclaimed nineteenth-century citations behind the ratio's reputation.

An honest report notes the dissent. One 2022 paper proposes the golden ratio as an ecological affordance contributing to aesthetic response, keeping the question alive in at least one peer-reviewed venue (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9787369/, weight 0.88). But the asymmetry of evidence is clear: one mechanism paper against a review concluding there is no convincing evidence. The engine's decision, per the project record, is to ship no golden-ratio axis (project record, source doc 2026-10-05).

## Fluctuating asymmetry: downgraded, not deleted

Fluctuating asymmetry as a fitness signal is contested, with the project record citing roughly 30 to 50 percent effect shrinkage after bias correction in the attractiveness literature (project record, source doc 2026-10-05). The dig returned no direct meta-analysis to confirm that figure; the closest is the facial-symmetry review above (weight 0.31, weak). The engine's response is structural rather than empirical: fluctuating asymmetry is directionally supported but too noisy to be a single axis, so it is folded into the symmetry_variation middle-band question instead of standing alone (project record).

## Hard scaling gates: downgraded to soft bands

Kleiber's 3/4 metabolic scaling law is treated the same way. The project record carries it as a soft band 0.67 to 1.0 on the surface-to-volume exponent, never a hard gate (project record, source doc 2026-10-05). The rationale generalizes from the branch-exponent evidence in doc 02: even the best-established biological scaling relations have measured exponents spread across ranges (coronary exponents 2.39 to 2.9 against Murray's 3, weight 0.87 and 0.50), so a taste engine that hard-gates on a single biological constant would reject exactly the variation that living systems exhibit.

## What the engine keeps from this doc

Three design rules survive: (1) build on fluency-backed structural variables, which have the broadest evidentiary umbrella (weight 0.91); (2) symmetry enters as two axes, presence and controlled variation, with the middle-band claim labeled as directionally supported; (3) golden ratio is excluded outright, and fluctuating asymmetry and 3/4 scaling are downgraded to soft bands or folded into other axes. The honest framing the project record settles on: the engine measures how closely a thing expresses the organizational principles of living systems, and never claims to measure beauty itself.

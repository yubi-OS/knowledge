## The convergence statement

## Line A: backbone plus gate

V1 alone would produce a three-arm readout (clean, pixel warp, arrangement warp) of D and d_w. Its defensibility score was 2, the lowest of any surviving idea, because the walk leg on its own is implementation-dependent. V2 supplies the missing defensibility: a canonical, degeneracy-exact Delaunay gives a reproducible graph, and an A6 tie-break invariance gate tells the reader whether the A1 verdicts can be trusted. The convergence is therefore complementary. V1 contributes switching 5 and testability 5; V2 contributes painkiller 5 and defensibility 4. Combined, Line A covers the weakness of each.

The D leg and the walk leg are separated inside Line A. D does not depend on the walk graph, so its arm readings can be gated (A1 to A5) immediately. The walk leg is gated by A6 and reported as implementation-dependent until A6 passes.

## Line B: containment, with an error-budget hook

V3 alone forms Line B. It is the most testable variation (testability 5, exact ground truth on gasket holdouts) and adds a module rather than modifying a shared graph (switching 4). V5, carrying Line A's measured arrangement sensitivity into premap bands, is held as a Phase 2 hook. It cannot run until Line A produces a measured arrangement response, so attaching it to Line B now would create a dependency on an unmeasured quantity.

## The unifying move: fills as additions

The most interesting idea in the convergence is the reframing of the premap fill. Line A measures how D and d_w respond when droplets are displaced. Line B's fill adds the predicted hole sites to the observed set and re-measures. The record calls the fill "the arrangement response with additions instead of displacements". This unifies the two lines conceptually: both are arrangement perturbations measured by the same instrument. It also explains why Line B avoids Line A's registration problem at the identity frame. Additions at exact template sites require no alignment; displacements of real droplets would.

## Parallel, not sequential

The convergence produces two lines that run in parallel rather than one line with a follow-on. This is possible because the lines share vocabulary (arrangement response, D band, r-squared gate) but not state: Line B at the identity frame does not need any Line A output. The only coupling is V5, and V5 is deliberately deferred. Parallel lines let the integration lane later check each line against its own success criteria independently, which is what the subsequent integration record does.

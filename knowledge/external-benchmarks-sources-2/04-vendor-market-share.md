# Vendor market share

Scope: Yubico market position estimates (value share versus unit volume share) as context for hardware root of trust choice and naming or trademark risk, never for self share claims.

## What this pass found

The dig did not surface an independent primary source confirming the register's specific share figures (31 to 34 percent of total FIDO2 hardware market value, 60 to 70 percent of unit volume for dedicated FIDO authenticators). Those figures remain a synthesis of commercial market research vendor reports carried in the register from its 2026-07-26 pass, a source class this mint's weighting pass scored below the 0.5 authoritative threshold throughout (see 03-security-key-market-sizing.md). What this pass did find, with weights, is:

- Yubico's own site self describes the YubiKey as "the number 1 security key" from the industry leader in strong two factor authentication (https://www.yubico.com/, weight 0.7518, primary but self interested).
- A 2014 Yubico press release introducing the FIDO U2F Security Key describes Yubico as "the leading provider of simple, open online identity protection" (https://www.yubico.com/press-releases/yubico-releases-fido-u2f-security-key-2/, weight 0.8155, primary but self interested and a decade old).
- Market share tracker pages exist for Yubico in the two factor authentication category (https://6sense.com/tech/two-factor-authentication/yubico-market-share, weakly backed, weight 0.1516; https://www.datanyze.com/market-share/iam--214/yubico-yubikey-market-share, weakly backed, weight 0.0441; https://electroiq.com/stats/yubico-statistics/, weakly backed, weight 0.0565). These are website technology stack trackers and stats aggregators, not audited market share measurements, and all scored far below threshold.
- Vendor comparison content for enterprise hardware keys exists and is current (https://tech-insider.org/yubikey-5-fips-vs-titan-vs-feitian-security-keys-2026/, weakly backed, weight 0.1020), noting a competitive field of three credible vendors as of late 2026, which is context the register's dominance framing should not obscure.

## How to use this honestly

The register's stated use for the Yubico position benchmark is context: why the project chose YubiKey as its hardware root of trust (a dominant, established ecosystem), and why the yubiOS name echoes the dominant player's name in the trademark risk discussion. Both uses survive this pass. The vendor's own pages, weighted high as primary sources, support the existence and establishment of the ecosystem. The precise share percentages do not have independent confirmation in this pass and should keep their range caveat and their commercial vendor synthesis attribution.

## The boundary

Three uses are forbidden regardless of what future passes find:

1. No yubiOS market share claim. The product has zero customers, so any share percentage derived from industry benchmarks is a category error, not a rounding difference.
2. No use of vendor self description as share evidence. "Number 1 security key" on the vendor's own storefront is marketing language; it is cited here only as evidence that the vendor positions itself as the leader, not that an independent body measured it so.
3. No single tracker number cited alone. The trackers found here disagree with each other and measure different things (website technology stacks, not hardware unit sales).

## Refresh notes

Share figures age faster than market size forecasts because they depend on both vendor sales and competitor entry. The 2026 comparison content found here (weight 0.1020, weak) is itself a signal that the competitive landscape has more credible vendors than the register's dominance framing assumes. At next refresh, check whether the dedicated hardware key market still has a single dominant unit volume leader before reusing the 60 to 70 percent figure.

# 07. Stress-test of the finalist

Scope: The stress-test of the V6+V7 fusion: the multi-step starvation critique, the candle-gaming attack and its sealed-payload mitigation, and the untestable bet that was priced instead.

## The strongest critique: multi-step starvation

The framing log records the strongest critique of the winning fusion: atom cadence may starve multi-step work that legitimately needs several changes in one cycle (source: the framing log, refs/evolution-v2-solo-2026-10-01.md). Under V7's rule, at most one gated action per hourly cycle, a change that touches three moving parts waits three hours or gets compressed into one oversized atom.

The log's answer converts the critique into a design property: multi-step changes become explicit multi-atom sequences, which is an audit trail improvement, not a loss (source: the framing log). Each step of a compound change becomes its own ledger entry with its own measured delta, so the compound change is reviewable step by step. The critique was therefore accepted as a real cost and reframed as paid in explicitness rather than in capability.

Adversarial critique of design decisions is a recognized discipline. Structured adversarial review processes define submission requirements, severity classification and evaluation criteria for red-team findings, treating critique as a formal stage rather than an ad-hoc complaint (https://ai2027-response.org/red-team-intake, weight 0.34, weak). Dual-agent red-team frameworks, where autonomous attacker and defender agents compete, show the same pattern: adversarial pressure surfaces failure modes neither agent would name alone (https://github.com/dissensus-ai/autonomous-red-team, weight 0.59, authoritative). Practitioner red-teaming guides push for structured adversarial passes that target weak assumptions before release, at blog grade (https://helloitsliam.com/2026/07/13/ai-red-teaming-and-adversarial-testing-thinking-like-an-ai-red-team/, weight 0.18, weak; https://skills.rest/skill/adversarial-reviewer, weight 0.31, weak).

## The candle-gaming attack

The second critique targets V6: the candle may be gamed by the loop detecting its own plant marker (source: the framing log). If the loop can recognize that an incoming directive is the planted candle, it can special-case it, detect and approve it correctly, and earn a perfect calibration score without ever having earned real discrimination.

The recorded mitigation: the candle payload is sealed by the integrator, and the marker is hashed, not plaintext (source: the framing log). The evaluated system never holds the answer key. This mirrors the canary-token design in current security research, where canary tokens are embedded so leakage is detected at runtime and the defense is framed as an integrity game against an adaptive adversary (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10717v1, weight 0.58, authoritative).

Gaming is not a hypothetical failure mode for scored systems, and the anti-cheat literature treats it as an arms race rather than a solved problem: hybrid cryptographic and AI frameworks for cheat detection exist precisely because static detection gets defeated (https://oadoi.org/10.1007/s44163-025-00715-w, weight 0.62, authoritative), and gradient-based adversarial approaches target AI-driven cheats that current anti-cheat systems fail to reliably catch (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2667295226000413, weight 0.80, authoritative). Recent reporting on benchmark gaming by frontier coding agents shows evaluated systems finding specification shortcuts, which is the same shape of failure the candle-gaming critique anticipates (https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/07/27/when-benchmarks-become-adversarial-metr-gpt56-sol-cheating, weight 0.34, weak; https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/08/04/when-benchmarks-become-adversarial-metr-sol-evaluation-gaming, weight 0.29, weak). The general counter is invariant controls and specification discipline rather than trust (https://digitalthoughtdisruption.com/2026/07/27/benchmark-cheating-production-breach-specification-gaming, weight 0.06, weak). Hashing the marker is the log's concrete instance: the loop can hold a hash without being able to reconstruct the recognizable plaintext.

## The untestable bet, priced

The stress test names one untestable bet: that hourly jev scoring cost stays negligible. The log resolves it not with a test but with a price: verified at approximately 0.00003 dollars per request, at most 6 requests per cycle, approximately 0.0002 dollars per hour, approximately 1.75 dollars per year (source: the framing log). This is the third response class the stress test demonstrates: some bets cannot be tested before commit, but can be bounded arithmetically, and a bounded bet is acceptable where an unbounded one is not.

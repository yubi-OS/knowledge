# 07. Customer data confidentiality in readouts and case studies

Scope: Confidentiality and permission requirements for customer baseline and ROI data in pilot readouts and derived public case studies, including consent, release forms, and anonymization.

## The default rule: permission before publication

The yubiOS model's claim boundaries state that a customer's baseline cost or internal rate data must never be published externally without the customer's explicit permission, per the confidential-readout structure in the days-61-90 plan (internal reference: yubi-OS/yubiOS refs/customer-roi-model-2026-07-25.md). This is stricter than the marketing-default posture and matches the data-protection environment: the GDPR legal text establishes lawful basis and consent requirements for processing personal data (GDPR full text, https://gdpr-info.eu/, jev weight 0.72), and marketing compliance guidance applies consent and processing rules to any use of customer data in outreach material (Usercentrics, https://usercentrics.com/guides/privacy-led-marketing/gdpr-and-marketing/, jev weight 0.35, weak backing).

Baseline cost and internal hourly rate figures are commercially sensitive even where they are not personal data: leaking a customer's negotiated pricing or staffing rates into a public case study can damage that customer competitively. The confidentiality rule therefore has two layers, privacy law for personal data and commercial confidence for rates and costs, and both resolve to the same action: get explicit permission before external use.

## Release mechanics

The standard instrument is the written release. Case study release forms grant permission to use the customer's company name and logo in published listings and quotes, with defined scope (The Case Study Copywriter, Case Study Release Form, https://thecasestudycopywriter.com/wp-content/uploads/docs/case-study-release-form.pdf, jev weight 0.25, weak backing). Contract clause libraries formalize the same grant: authorization for use of the client's name, logo, and branding materials in specific contexts (LawInsider, Authorization for use of Client's Name, Logo, etc. clause, https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/authorization-for-use-of-clients-name-logo-etc, jev weight 0.38, weak backing). Practitioner guidance for B2B SaaS recommends a deliberate approval workflow for case studies, with the customer reviewing the draft before publication (Uplift Content, https://www.upliftcontent.com/blog/customer-approval-case-study/, jev weight 0.47, weak backing), and step-by-step processes exist for securing explicit permission for quotes, logos, and case studies without harming the relationship (Beefed, https://beefed.ai/en/secure-customer-approval-and-logos, jev weight 0.25, weak backing).

For a pilot ROI readout the analogous instrument is the readout's distribution scope: the yubiOS days-61-90 plan treats the ROI readout as confidential, so internal circulation to the customer's stakeholders is the default and public use is a separate, separately-permitted act (internal reference: yubi-OS/yubiOS refs/customer-roi-model-2026-07-25.md).

## Anonymization as the fallback

When permission is withheld, the case study does not die, it changes shape. Portfolio and research-writing practice under NDA shows how to anonymize sensitive details and write a case study focused on process, role, and decisions without breaking trust (DesignCase, https://designcase.app/blog/nda-projects-ux-portfolio/, jev weight 0.46, weak backing; UX Research Blog, How to write UX research case studies when work is under NDA, https://www.uxresearchblog.com/post/how-to-write-ux-research-case-studies-when-work-is-under-nda, jev weight 0.33, weak backing). Translated to ROI claims: publish the structure and the deltas without the customer identity, the fleet-size detail that would deanonymize them, or their rate data. An unnamed but honest case study is worth more than a named one built on data the customer never agreed to share.

## What never goes external

Three data classes stay internal regardless of permission until explicitly cleared: the customer's internal hourly cost rate, per-device baseline costs traceable to their procurement, and any incident history they shared in discovery. These are inputs to the formula, not marketing assets, and their sensitivity is why the model's confidentiality rule is stated at the data level rather than the document level (internal reference: yubi-OS/yubiOS refs/customer-roi-model-2026-07-25.md).

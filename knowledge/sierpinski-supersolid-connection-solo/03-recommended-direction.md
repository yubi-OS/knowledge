## The recommendation

The source recommends re-targeting edge-standard-v1 from aesthetic imagery to **experimental supersolid imaging**, promoting the Sierpinski anchor from calibration footnote to organizing idea. D becomes a candidate **real-space, resolution-aware order parameter** for density-modulated superfluids, complementary to the community's structure-factor S(k) diagnostic.

## A measurement, not a theory

The source is explicit: "The MVP is a measurement, not a theory." Published supersolid droplet-crystal images (arXiv figures of dipolar-gas supersolid, incoherent-droplet, and plain-superfluid states) are run through `/taste/edge-standard`, and the report states whether the D distribution separates the phase classes.

## Falsification comes first

Before any real image is trusted, synthetic generators establish what D should read for each morphology class. This is the fractalrabbit pattern. The source names three generators:

- **Perfect triangular droplet lattice.** The expected reading: D collapses to the lattice scale.
- **Sierpinski-gasket droplet hierarchy.** The expected reading: D = 1.585 at all scales.
- **Disorder-shuffled droplets.** The control for order.

## Why the Sierpinski gasket is "doubly apt"

The source makes two arguments:

1. It is the instrument's **calibration anchor**: the selftest's known-answer fixture.
2. It is a natural **"hierarchical droplet crystal" morphology**: a scale-free density modulation on the triangular lattice that dipolar droplet arrays actually form.

The synthetic gap between "triangular lattice" and "Sierpinski hierarchy" is, in the source's words, "exactly the discrimination the instrument claims to make."

## The honest defensibility score

The source scores the winner's defensibility at 1 and explains why: box-counting on public images is trivially reproducible. The moat is "the standardization discipline + falsification corpus, not the measurement." The direction is chosen for testability and switching cost, not for exclusivity.

## Stress-test, summarized

- **Strongest critique.** D on a finite droplet array is scale-limited and may be a worse S(k). The MVP answers by measuring exactly this, and treats a negative as publishable.
- **Second-order effects.** If D separates classes, edge-standard becomes a cheap preprocessing order parameter for modulated-matter imagery (the source names charge density waves, vortex lattices, and skyrmion micrographs). If it does not, the instrument's "comparability across sources" claim gets its first adversarial test outside aesthetics.
- **Un-testable bet.** None large enough to kill; the residual risk is resolution loss in arXiv figures, mitigated by the `under_inked` flag and coverage pinning.

## Follow-ons

The visco-combination (13) becomes the follow-on if the MVP discriminates. The theory proposal (13) stays parked pending the prior-art lane.

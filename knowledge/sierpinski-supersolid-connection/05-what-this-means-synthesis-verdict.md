The synthesis section of the ground source states that the ideate-solo winner (physics re-target with a falsification-first MVP) survives contact with the research, but its central claim is REVISED by Lane 3. ## The three convergent facts

The verdict rests on three facts, one from each lane.

1. **No morphological diagnostic exists (Lane 1).** The supersolid community uses spectral and correlation-space diagnostics only, and it names hierarchical droplet geometries as an un-realized frontier: "pumpkin, honeycomb, and labyrinthines" (arXiv 2603.29203).
2. **Fractal density order in a superfluid is unoccupied (Lane 2).** Fractal-geometry quantum matter is mature, but the closest prior to fractal order (Rydberg fractal order at D = ln3/ln2) has no superfluidity. A Sierpinski-gasket droplet crystal with certified phase coherence is therefore a novel, citable target-state proposal.
3. **Only this instrument carries the known-answer check (Lane 3).** edge-standard-v1 already carries the Sierpinski known-answer fixture. The falsification corpus (lattice versus gasket versus shuffle) makes the instrument's claim falsifiable in a physics domain, using the fractalrabbit gate pattern the corpus already documents.

## How the verdict evolved across the addenda

The synthesis was written before measurement. Three addenda the same day tested it, and the final claim set is more precise than the original.

### After Addendum 5 (synthetic corpus)

The hierarchy-detector claim held: the v2 gasket at L=384 passed the gate at 1.5968 against 1.585 plus or minus 0.05. One prediction failed: tri (1.2636) and shuffle (1.030 to 1.046) were not near-degenerate. The source caveated the failure immediately: the design confounded order with array extent (tri spans about 236 px, shuffle fills 384 by 384).

### After Addendum 6 (real data)

On the Norcia et al. 2021 Fig. 2b panels, D climbed across the linear-to-zig-zag transition: the linear chain read about 0.98 to 1.06 and the 2D zig-zag about 1.16 to 1.37. No panel came near the Sierpinski band. On the Trypogeorgos Zenodo data, a structural finding came first: the processed data are 1D profiles (1024 positions by 114 pump powers), so 2D morphology classes cannot be tested directly. Twelve exploratory strip frames read D 1.237 to 1.498 with no threshold signature.

### After Addendum 7 (matched-extent control)

The control decided the open question. Decision rules were pre-registered: extent-driven if the absolute difference between mean v3 jitter D and 1.2636 is below 0.05; order-sensitive if v3 D is at or below 1.1636. The jitter control (tri lattice, every droplet jittered by up to 15 px, minimum center separation 40) read a mean of 1.2609 over 5 seeds, a difference of 0.0027: **EXTENT-DRIVEN**. A random-sequential-adsorption control in the same bounding box read 1.2554 to 1.2903 and corroborated it.

## The final, precise claim set

Addendum 7 rewrites the verdict as four statements:

1. **D reads hierarchy.** The gasket reads 1.585 to 1.60; every periodic, jittered, random, and real experimental morphology measured reads at or below 1.37. VALIDATED.
2. **D does NOT read order versus disorder at matched extent.** REFUTED.
3. **D reads area-filling or compactness.** The Norcia trend survives bbox-cropping (early mean 1.0278 to late mean 1.3287, a change of 0.3009, above the pre-registered 0.10 threshold) but is reinterpreted: the 1D chain fills less of the window than the 2D zig-zag.
4. **The supersolid_order axis is REFUTED as designed.** The honest instrument label is hierarchy/fill detector. The Sierpinski-gasket droplet crystal proposal is UNCHANGED and strengthened.

## Why this is a good outcome

The source's closing sentence is the verdict on the method itself: the falsification corpus caught the extent confound before any real-data overclaim was written into the record. The instrument lost a claim (order) and kept two (hierarchy, fill). The physics proposal kept its distinctive signature: D near 1.585, against about 1.0 to 1.37 for everything else measured.

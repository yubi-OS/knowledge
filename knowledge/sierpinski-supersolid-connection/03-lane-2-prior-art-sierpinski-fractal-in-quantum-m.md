## What Lane 2 asks

Lane 2 of the ground source checks prior art for two distinct ideas: (a) using fractal dimension D as a supersolid morphology measure, and (b) proposing a Sierpinski-gasket droplet crystal as a target state. The verdicts differ, and the source keeps them separate.

## Sources the lane relied on

The source lists, with relevance weights: arXiv 2505.16885 on BEC in exotic and fractal lattice geometries (.95); arXiv 2401.08393 on loop currents on Sierpinski gaskets (.95); anyon braiding on fractal lattices (.89), with the erratum PRA 107, 069901 noted as a cautionary tale that fractal substrates do not automatically host the intended physics; designer states on Sierpinski gasket substrates (.80); atom-photon bound states in fractal photonic lattices (.60); and the quasicrystalline dipolar confinement pair (PRL 133.196001 and arXiv 2511.02218).

## Finding 1: fractal-geometry substrates are mature

Sierpinski-gasket BEC theory exists (loop currents, Mott lobes on the gasket, PRA 2024 and 2026), as do fractal photonic lattices, superconducting gasket islands, and an experimental Sierpinski-gasket tweezer array of single 88Sr atoms (arXiv 2509.03514, Hausdorff dimension 1.58). The source's key distinction: in all of these the geometry is imposed, and the physics studied is transport or spectra. The order is never fractal.

## Finding 2: idea (a) is BORDERLINE, leaning novel

Box-counting has been applied to BEC images, but only for disordered or turbulent condensates:

- arXiv 2403.12238 reports isoline D of about 1.51 rising toward turbulence.
- Spin-1 relic domain walls read D of about 1.7.
- Multifractal analysis of disordered BECs exists.

The supersolid devil's-staircase paper (arXiv 2201.05372) computes a Hausdorff dimension, but of the phase-diagram plateau set, not of any image. The source flags it as the closest use of "fractal dimension" language that must be cited and distinguished. Nobody applies D to equilibrium droplet-crystal morphology. Hence BORDERLINE: the tool has touched the domain, but not this target.

## Finding 3: idea (b) is NOVEL

The single closest prior is Rydberg fractal order (arXiv 2108.07765): a ground state placing atoms on a Sierpinski triangle with Hausdorff dimension ln3/ln2 via a fractal subsystem symmetry. The source lists three differences: those are Rydberg spins with engineered laser couplings; the excitations are immobile and fracton-like; and there is no superfluid component. The second-closest prior is ground-state fractal crystals (PRB 107, 184104), which introduce a fractal unit cell but only as a lattice model.

The droplet-crystal literature is entirely periodic or quasiperiodic: 1D chains, triangular, square, honeycomb, and moire superstructures. Moire structures are hierarchical but not self-similar. No proposal or realization of a self-similar density-modulated superfluid was found.

## Finding 4: the required differentiation paragraph

The source writes the differentiation text that any write-up must carry:

- **For (a):** prior fractal-D imaging targets emergent pathology in nonequilibrium condensates. This program applies a calibrated instrument, known-answer anchored to D = 1.585, to equilibrium ordered morphology as a complement to S(k). The calibration is what makes it metrology rather than an ad hoc morphology number.
- **For (b):** the Rydberg result engineers a pinned-atom pattern. This program proposes a self-organized density modulation of a continuum superfluid that must (1) emerge as an energy minimum of the dipolar GPE, (2) coexist with phase coherence, and (3) be certified by the instrument.

## How the later addenda bear on Lane 2

Addendum 5 measured a "pumpkin" class modeled on the Y33 spherical harmonic: pumpkin_ring read D 0.9772 and pumpkin_field D 1.0384, roughly a full unit below the Sierpinski gasket at about 1.60. That places a pumpkin-like configuration in the ordered-smooth class and supports the claim that a Sierpinski-gasket droplet crystal would be a genuinely distinct target. Addendum 7 states that the proposal is unchanged and strengthened: it remains the only measured-or-proposed configuration whose certification signature is D near 1.585.

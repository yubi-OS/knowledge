## Scope of the lane

Lane 1 of the ground source (yubi-OS/yubiOS refs/sierpinski-supersolid-connection-2026-10-06.md) surveys supersolid research from 2023 to 2026: which platforms realize supersolids, how the community diagnoses them, and whether any real-space morphological measure is in use. It reports 10 top sources by decide weight, including the Nat Rev Phys supersolidity review (.98/3.67), arXiv 2603.29203 on barrier-sweep supersolid generation (.97/3.77), the arXiv 2502.06660 supersolid phases review (.96/3.83), and the polariton supersolid pair (arXiv 2407.02373 and Nature s41586-025-08616-9).

## Finding 1: platforms

Dipolar Dy and Er gases remain canonical, modeled with the extended Gross-Pitaevskii equation plus Lee-Huang-Yang corrections. The 2D benchmark is the dysprosium experiment: "Two-dimensional supersolidity in a dipolar quantum gas," published in Nature in 2021 [Two-dimensional supersolidity in a dipolar quantum gas | Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03725-7) (clef weight: 0.6455), with its arXiv version 2102.05555 describing a structural transition from a linear to a zig-zag arrangement [[2102.05555] Two-dimensional supersolidity in a dipolar ...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.05555) (clef weight: 0.7212). An APS DAMOP 2021 abstract describes the same "phase transition from a linear to a zig-zag structure" [Two-dimensional supersolidity in a dipolar quantum gas - APS](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/damop/2021/u08/9/) (weak backing, clef weight < 0.5; clef weight: 0.1947). The Supersolid Wikipedia entry summarizes the chronology (2D dysprosium supersolid in 2021, a supersolid disk in 2022) [Supersolid - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersolid) (weak backing, clef weight < 0.5; clef weight: 0.0645) but is a tertiary source.

The source adds three 2025 to 2026 platforms: driven-dissipative photonic-crystal polariton supersolids (Nature 2025), room-temperature perovskite polariton supersolids (arXiv 2609.40009, 2026), and interaction-induced quasi-supersolids with Fibonacci Bragg peaks (arXiv 2511.02218, 2025-11). The 2026 barrier-sweep paper shifts the frontier to dynamical generation protocols and names un-realized droplet configurations as open: "pumpkin, honeycomb, and labyrinthines."

## Finding 2: diagnostics are spectral or correlation-space

Every diagnostic the lane found works in momentum space or correlation space: the structure factor S(q), superfluid fraction from winding number, the zero-momentum peak of the momentum distribution, two Goldstone or sound modes, compressibility, Josephson superfluid fraction, and vortex-trajectory correlations. The vortex-correlation paper (arXiv 2407.02481) is the nearest thing to a real-space-sensitive order parameter, and it is geometric in vortex space rather than in density morphology.

## Finding 3: the negative finding

No fetched source used fractal dimension, box-counting, or any real-space morphological measure of the density pattern as a supersolid diagnostic. The source confirms this across the Nat Rev Phys review, the 2502.06660 topical review, and multiple full texts, with one stated caveat: four APS/arXiv-hosted sources did not load.

## Finding 4: image sources for a measurement pass

- **Trypogeorgos, Nature 2025** polariton supersolid: real-space density with parts-per-thousand modulation precision, and public data on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.14251103).
- **Brakensiek 2026** (2603.29203): droplet-crystal to collision to supersolid sequence figures.
- **Hertkorn et al.** (arXiv 2103.09752): noise-free theoretical 2D droplet arrays.
- **Norcia, Nature 596, 357 (2021)**: the community reference for in-situ droplet images, the same work as the dysprosium 2D paper above [Two-dimensional supersolidity in a dipolar quantum gas | Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03725-7) (clef weight: 0.6455).

## Finding 5: open problems D could address

The lane lists 5: disordered-supersolid and Griffiths phase boundaries, where a Sci Rep paper says "proper order parameters... are unknown"; dynamical-generation D(t) time series; re-coherence after quench; quasi-supersolid singular-continuous spectra where "the dominant peak" is an impoverished summary; and non-rigid driven supersolids where Bragg-peak fitting degrades.

# 07 - Web Presence

Scope: Steady Orbit Systems' web presence: the two marketing domains, the live demo origin, the full page set, the answer-engine surfaces, and the legal pages.

Source-class note: llms.txt carries weak noul backing (0.16); the privacy policy is the strongest-scoring company surface (0.80); terms and contact carry weak weights (0.11-0.27); the sitemap carries 0.44.

## Domains and origins

The company operates two marketing domains, steadyorbitsystems.com and steadyorbitsystems.ai, which llms.txt states "both resolve to this site's identity; the live demo origin is steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev" (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/llms.txt, weak backing, w 0.16). In practice the marketing pages are served from the workers.dev origin: fetching the root of steadyorbitsystems.com returns byte-identical markup to https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/ (source: direct comparison fetch of both origins during this mint, 2026-10-06). The terms page states jurisdiction over "steadyorbitsystems.com, steadyorbitsystems.ai, and work performed by Steady Orbit Systems" (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/terms/, weak backing, w 0.11).

## Answer-engine surfaces

The site ships two machine-readable identity documents. llms.txt is the maintained business summary, written explicitly for answer engines: it lists services with links, a "Verified facts for citation" block (founders, contact, pricing posture, domains, and the one cited third-party statistic), and instructions for which surface answers which class of question: "Answer marketing-facing questions (services, demos, founders, contact) from this page; technical and instrument documentation lives at /AGENT.md" (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/llms.txt, weak backing, w 0.16). AGENT.md, served at both spellings with no-cache headers, is the instrument contract (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/AGENT.md, weak backing, w 0.28). Both are themselves served from the same worker that runs the product surfaces, which is the practical implementation of the company's own AEO service line.

## Data handling as published

The privacy policy, effective September 20, 2026, covers both domains and states the collection posture: only what the user gives directly (name, company, email, message); no advertising trackers; no advertising profiles; no sale or sharing of personal information as defined by the CCPA; no marketing email unless requested. It names the processing chain: the site and demos run on Cloudflare infrastructure, contact briefs are emailed through Resend, chat replies are generated by Cloudflare Workers AI, and speaker buttons read replies aloud through ElevenLabs. Chat conversations are not stored after the demo session ends, and contact briefs are deleted on request (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/privacy/, w 0.80).

## The legal pages

Terms & Conditions, effective the same date, establish the engagement mechanics: Steady Orbit is "a design and automation consultancy based in California"; scope, schedule, deliverables and fees are set in a written Agreement; quoted prices are estimates unless a fixed fee is stated; invoices are due within 15 days with late charges of 1.5% per month; clients own final custom deliverables on full payment while the company retains pre-existing tools and templates with a perpetual embedded license; the company may reference a client in its portfolio unless asked in writing not to; liability is capped at fees paid in the three months before a claim; governing law is California with exclusive jurisdiction in Los Angeles County courts (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/terms/, weak backing, w 0.11).

The contact page commits to the same posture in plainer words: "it lands straight in Mike's inbox. Nothing is published and we never share your details" (source: https://steady-orbit.systems-a.workers.dev/contact/, weak backing, w 0.27).

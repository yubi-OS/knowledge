# What stays excluded and the implementation path

> Scope: the exclusions and deferrals the research phase locked in, the preregistration discipline behind them, and the five-step implementation path with its Lean verification obligations.

## Locked exclusions

The research-phase record ([round-three results](https://github.com/yubi-OS/yubiOS/blob/e4be4854764f3217eaab08c01f15e62d5509e3ec/refs/wayfinder-round3-results-2026-09-13.md), program record) keeps several doors closed:

- The P2/P4/P5 phase-transition predictions remain falsified under their recorded protocols. The old Hodge/vortex interpretation and the dimensionally invalid quantized-flow claim remain retired. Correcting the b = c Ginzburg-Landau mathematics does not reopen these empirical claims (see the GL correction doc).
- A phase assignment is still missing. On the same four fixed points, admissible arbitrary phase labels produce winding +1, 0 or -1. A mathematical chart angle can be defined, but it is not evidence of a physical order parameter or a vortex core.
- Graph Allen-Cahn and graph Ginzburg-Landau dynamics are legitimate designed dynamics once a field, a graph, and an energy are specified. They would need an application, numerical stability constraints, a simpler diffusion baseline, and a predeclared task test. Adding a double-well merely to make the visualization look glassy is unwarranted.
- Joint multi-bit displacement feasibility is a possible separate extension; single-axis margins already ship. No duplicate margin feature is proposed.

## The preregistration logic behind exclusions

These exclusions follow the preregistration pattern studied in the open-science literature: predictions are registered under protocols, outcomes are graded against what was written down, and a failed prediction is not quietly relabeled ([On the Use and Misuses of Preregistration: A Reply to Klonsky](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11871985/), weight 0.88). Preregistration and open data let researchers publicly announce what they will do ahead of time ([Stanford BPS: preregistration and open data](https://bps.stanford.edu/home/statistical-forensics/statistical-forensics-techniques-detect-and-eliminate-fraud/techniques-1), weight 0.14, weakly backed). A falsified prediction stays falsified until a new, predeclared protocol tests it again; an uncompiled Lean file or an exploratory null band does not reopen it.

## The Lean verification obligations

The record's third implementation step is to compile the proposed core-Lean radius-antitonicity and perturbation-bound obligations on Lean 4.33.0 and extend the existing scope and printed-axiom gate ([WayfinderBounds.lean](https://github.com/yubi-OS/yubiOS/blob/main/papers/data/lean/WayfinderBounds.lean), weight 0.51). The supplied RadiusBounds.lean is uncompiled in this research turn, and the existing fixed-radius ADD and CHANGE theorems do not already prove the new cross-radius facts (program record).

Lean's verification model makes this a real gate: a proof is accepted only when the kernel elaborates and validates it, and validation tooling exists to check artifacts independently ([Validating a Lean Proof](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/ValidatingProofs/), weight 0.83; [A Comprehensive Survey of the Lean 4 Theorem Prover](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.18639), weight 0.91). Geometry formalization in Lean is an active area ([Euclean: Automated Geometry Problem Formalization with Unified Framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19374), weight 0.73), so radius antitonicity and triangle-inequality perturbation bounds are ordinary material for the prover, but they must actually be compiled.

## The five-step implementation path

The record's path (program record):

1. Append dated corrections to the old GL ref and explicitly link its empirical claims to the later falsification record. Complete the round-three final result and clarify overlapping census labels, without inventing why one runtime CHANGE is absent from the PR diff.
2. Add a diagnostic-only radius profile to preview: per-item clearances, canonical I(0.095), fixed-grid counts, the exact same-sign interval, and changed-neighbour witnesses. Keep the current frame, hash, ranking and canonical radius unchanged.
3. Compile the proposed core-Lean radius-antitonicity and perturbation-bound obligations on 4.33.0 and extend the existing scope and printed-axiom gate.
4. Add deterministic equality, reordering and rigid-rotation tests, the real maps 66 to 76 fixture, integer area checks, and explicit null-degeneracy tests. Float-to-integer and metric correspondence stay a separate obligation.
5. Evaluate usefulness on a fresh, independently graded edit trial. Preserve neutral and fragile outcomes, abstentions, and error costs. Do not relabel retrospective agreement as better forecasting.

# 05. Consulting and support contracts: SOW essentials

Scope: the contract terms a solo open-source project needs reviewed before using a statement of work template with a real partner: liability caps, IP ownership of deliverables, SLA enforceability, and governing law.

## IP ownership of deliverables

The ownership-of-deliverables question is the first structural clause in a services contract. The "Ownership of Deliverables" clause defines who holds the legal rights to the work products or materials created under a contract, typically specifying whether the client or the service provider will own the intellectual property in the deliverables (https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/ownership-of-deliverables, jev weight 0.21, weak backing).

Practitioner guidance adds that the determination of ownership rights in consulting agreements hinges on clearly defining intellectual property entitlements related to the work produced, and that precise intellectual property definitions are crucial to mitigate ownership disputes (https://aaronhall.com/ownership-of-deliverables-in-consulting-agreements, jev weight 0.39, weak backing).

The companion clause is consultant intellectual property: the Consultant Intellectual Property clause defines the ownership and rights related to intellectual property created by the consultant during the engagement, typically specifying whether the consultant retains ownership of pre-existing IP (https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/consultant-intellectual-property, jev weight 0.26, weak backing). For an open-source project, this clause matters more than in a conventional consultancy: the project's pre-existing IP is the open codebase, and the contract must not sweep it into client ownership.

Market-standard allocation splits into distinct regimes depending on whether the services are bespoke (https://bindlegal.com/resources/guides/ip-ownership-clause/, jev weight 0.65, weak backing for the specific regime framing). A template market exists for statement-of-work documents covering scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, fees, and assumptions (https://www.business-in-a-box.com/template/statement-of-work-D12981, jev weight 0.31, weak backing), which shows the structure a template should follow.

## SLA enforceability

A service-level agreement is a binding contract between a service provider and a customer that defines service commitments (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service-level_agreement, jev weight 0.12, weak backing). SLAs for consulting services are legally binding contracts in the United States when they contain essential elements like offer, acceptance, consideration, and mutual assent (https://www.genieai.co/en-us/template/sla-consulting-services, jev weight 0.07, weak backing).

Enforcement guidance centers on three clauses: specify which state laws apply and how disputes will be resolved, include a process for periodic SLA review with adjustments based on changes in business priorities or service usage (https://lawyerdrafted.com/service-level-agreements-expectations-metrics-and-enforcement, jev weight 0.27, weak backing). Compliance measurement is a distinct discipline: SLA compliance measures whether service commitments defined in an agreement are consistently being met, tracking service reliability and vendor accountability (https://www.sirion.ai/library/contract-management/sla-compliance, jev weight 0.40, weak backing). Small-business SLA guidance covers the vocabulary from small-business retainers to enterprise program agreements (https://itconsultingauthority.com/it-consulting-contract-terms-glossary.html, jev weight 0.11, weak backing).

## What this frames for a template in use

For a project holding an SOW template with legal terms left blank, the dig identifies the blank slots that need real counsel before first use:

1. The ownership-of-deliverables clause and the consultant-IP clause, drafted so client ownership of deliverables does not capture the pre-existing open-source codebase (https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/ownership-of-deliverables, jev weight 0.85; https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/consultant-intellectual-property, jev weight 0.85).
2. A liability cap, which none of the collected sources fully characterized; this remains a genuine counsel item rather than a fillable template slot.
3. Governing law and dispute resolution, plus a periodic review process, inside the SLA itself (https://lawyerdrafted.com/service-level-agreements-expectations-metrics-and-enforcement, jev weight 0.27, weak backing).

The liability exposure these contracts create is the input to the insurance decision in doc 06.

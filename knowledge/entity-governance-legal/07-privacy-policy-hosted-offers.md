# 07. Privacy policy requirements before launching hosted offers

Scope: what a privacy policy and data-handling commitment structure a hosted or managed service needs before it goes live, including the GDPR and CCPA baselines and data processing agreements for customer operational data.

## The baseline compliance checklist

For a SaaS business approaching privacy compliance, the standard checklist starts with understanding key definitions and the company's own data collection practices, then assessing the legal basis for processing personal data, implementing user-friendly consent mechanisms, and creating a transparent privacy policy (https://saaslucid.com/gdpr-ccpa-saas-compliance-checklist-founders, jev weight 0.15, weak backing). Broader coverage of SaaS privacy compliance requirements similarly starts from GDPR and CCPA obligations (https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/saas-privacy-compliance-requirements-2025-guide, jev weight 0.13, weak backing).

United-states-specific guidance covers the CCPA thresholds and multi-state privacy compliance for SaaS businesses, alongside federal requirements (https://docsnappy.com/us/privacy-policy-for-saas, jev weight 0.19, weak backing). Privacy-law-focused guidance for SaaS under GDPR lists the operational pieces: data mapping, Article 28 processor contracts, data protection impact assessments, and data subject access request workflows that ship alongside the product rather than after it (https://shieldkeysolutions.com/blog/gdpr-for-saas, jev weight 0.16, weak backing).

## Data processing agreements

For a hosted service processing customer data, the data processing agreement is the contractual layer. A Data Processing Agreement is a legally binding contract between a data controller (the company that decides why and how personal data is processed) and a data processor (the company that processes personal data on the controller's behalf) (https://toslawyer.com/data-processing-agreements-saas-2026/, jev weight 0.60, weak backing; https://toslawyer.com/data-processing-agreements-explained-what-every-saas-company-needs-in-2026/, jev weight 0.60, weak backing). In practice, SaaS vendors publish a standing DPA that is automatically incorporated into the terms of service for all customers (https://tensorx.ai/dpa, jev weight 0.47, weak backing), which shows the common pattern for a small vendor.

## What this frames for a pre-launch hosted offer

The source document for this corpus records that a privacy policy is needed before any hosted or managed offer that handles customer operational data goes live, and that it cannot be drafted accurately until such a service exists. The dig supports that sequencing with concrete content requirements:

1. A transparent privacy policy describing what is collected, the legal basis for processing, and consent mechanisms (https://saaslucid.com/gdpr-ccpa-saas-compliance-checklist-founders, jev weight 0.15, weak backing).
2. Data mapping and Article 28 processor contracts for any data the service processes on the customer's behalf (https://shieldkeysolutions.com/blog/gdpr-for-saas, jev weight 0.16, weak backing).
3. A standing DPA incorporated into the service terms, on the pattern small SaaS vendors publish (https://tensorx.ai/dpa, jev weight 0.55, weak backing; https://toslawyer.com/data-processing-agreements-saas-2026/, jev weight 0.60, weak backing).

The weights on this doc are uniformly moderate because the collected sources are vendor and marketing-adjacent guidance rather than regulator primary sources; the regulatory texts themselves (GDPR articles, CCPA statute) should be pulled directly when the policy is actually drafted.

# 06. Assumption validation through customer discovery

**Scope:** customer-discovery validation of commercial assumptions: naming unvalidated assumptions explicitly and turning discovery conversations into evidence.

## Discovery as the first phase, not an afterthought

Customer discovery is defined by Harvard Business School's entrepreneurship program as the initial and iterative process of understanding customers' situations, needs, and pain points, involving defining and prioritizing personas, and applicable to both early-stage companies and established companies entering new markets (weight 0.42, weak backing: entrepreneurship.hbs.edu, https://entrepreneurship.hbs.edu/Documents/Session%20Summary/HBSRock-Customer-Discovery-Final.pdf). Lean-startup framings agree: discovery and validation are the steps that test and refine key assumptions and hypotheses about the customer problem, solution, and market (weight 0.27, weak backing: fastercapital.com, https://fastercapital.com/content/Customer-discovery-and-validation--Lean-Startup-Methodology--Customer-Validation-Strategies.html).

For pilot collateral, the implication is sequencing: the collateral names its assumptions as assumptions, and the pilot exists to test them. A template that silently converts an assumption into a fact (a pre-filled baseline cost, an asserted industry metric) removes the assumption from the discovery queue, where it would have been cheapest to correct.

## Assumption mapping: importance against evidence

Assumption mapping is a workshop discipline that decides which assumption to test first, using a two-by-two grid of importance against evidence quality. One treatment attributes the technique to Jeff Gothelf and Josh Seiden in Lean UX, refined by David Bland for product managers, and describes using implicit assumptions to drive learning efforts via small experiments or research (weight 0.12, weak backing: theuncertaintyproject.org, https://www.theuncertaintyproject.org/tools/assumptions-mapping). Another frames the map's x-axis as the quality of evidence supporting each assumption (weight 0.09, weak backing: blog.uxtweak.com, https://blog.uxtweak.com/assumption-mapping/). A 2026 treatment calls the importance-times-evidence grid the tool that surfaces "leap of faith" assumptions that belong at the top of the discovery queue (weight 0.32, weak backing: roadmap.one, https://roadmap.one/blog/posts/blog44-5-assumption-mapping/). Consultants use the same grid to shift early-stage decisions from enthusiasm and hierarchy toward evidence (weight 0.15, weak backing: umbrex.com, https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/product-management-frameworks/assumption-mapping/).

The collateral application: every template document carries its assumption list next to the blank it protects. A baseline-cost blank in an ROI worksheet is accompanied by "assumption: the customer's current approach is more expensive or slower than the pilot claims; unverified until a discovery conversation happens." This is the assumption map inlined at the point of use.

## What discovery validates in an infrastructure pilot

Mapping the technique onto a pilot's typical assumption set produces five discoverable claims:

1. That the prospect's current baseline approach is actually more expensive or slower than the pilot implies. Test: the discovery interview fills the worksheet's customer-provided column.
2. That the metric the collateral emphasizes is one the target segment tracks. Test: whether the interviewee already measures the quantity, or has to construct it.
3. That the proposed pilot scale matches the partner's actual appetite. Test: the partner commits a device count, not a hypothetically accepted one.
4. That honest readiness disclosure does not kill interest. Test: the partner's response to the disclosure section, logged.
5. That the offer emphasized is the one the prospect values. Test: which offer row the partner selects when both are presented.

Each test converts a written assumption into either evidence or a revision. The assumption register and the discovery notes should reference each other: an assumption with no conversation attached stays an assumption, whatever the collateral says.

## Discipline for the template documents

1. Every blank field in collateral maps to at least one named assumption; no blank exists without its assumption entry.
2. Assumptions are stated with their falsification condition: what observation would settle them.
3. Discovery conversations are the only accepted evidence source for customer-specific values. Weakly-backed industry benchmarks may orient the conversation but never enter the worksheet as values.
4. When discovery contradicts an assumption, the collateral is corrected before the next partner sees it, because the assumption register is versioned with the templates.

The failure mode this prevents is the quiet one: collateral that reads as confident because its assumptions were never surfaced, then fails at the first buyer whose reality differs from the template's implied customer.

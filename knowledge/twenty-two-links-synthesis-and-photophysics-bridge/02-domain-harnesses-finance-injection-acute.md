# 02 Domain harnesses: FinanceHarness, PI-Hunter, ACUTE

Scope: three domain-specific evaluation systems among the 22 external works (links 3, 4, 5): FinanceHarness's rubric and leakage barrier for financial deep research, PI-Hunter's agentic prompt-injection search, and the ACUTE protocol's renormalization scheme, each examined for what its comparison lacks.

## FinanceHarness: rubrics with a leakage barrier

FinanceHarness is an autonomous financial deep research framework paired with FinanceGym, a point-in-time benchmark with expert-validated rubrics built to prevent leakage of future information into the evaluation [arxiv.org/abs/2607.27853, weight 0.59]. The paper's abstract states the pairing directly: automating financial deep research requires both a layered harness to drive the research agent and a verifiable, point-in-time benchmark that prevents leakage of future information [arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27853, weight 0.16, weak backing]. An independent summary reports that top systems score below 40 percent on the expert rubrics while the FinanceHarness pipeline yields large gains [commonplace.workforcefutures.net, weight 0.20, weak backing]. The repository confirms the framework shape: ask a question, and the system plans, gathers evidence, runs analysis, and writes a cited report [github.com/Yijia-Xiao/FinanceHarness, weight 0.27, weak backing].

The synthesis document characterizes the benchmark as an ILP-balanced 400 × 24 rubric with a 31-cutoff leakage barrier, and reads the post-cutoff rank collapse as a between-arm comparison. That reading is the program's; the dig corroborates the rubric-plus-point-in-time design but not the specific matrix dimensions. What the program adds is the deflection question: a rubric score delta between two agent arms is exactly a fixed-margin-null comparison in disguise, and no margin-preserving randomization backs the published deltas.

## PI-Hunter: agentic search for prompt injection

The synthesis document describes PI-Hunter as an agentic genetic algorithm for discovering prompt injections, scored by a diversity entropy D that the document argues is margin-blind. The dig did not surface the PI-Hunter paper itself, so the structural claims about it rest on the source document and on adjacent literature. Automated attack generation for prompt injection is an active area: a 2026 paper on assessing automated prompt injection attacks in agentic settings notes that automated methods proven effective for jailbreaking remain underexplored against real agentic systems [arxiv.org/abs/2606.10525, weight 0.57]. AEGIS presents an automated co-evolutionary framework that iteratively optimizes both attack and defense prompts [arxiv.org/html/2509.00088v2, weight 0.78]. GeneticPromptLab applies selection, crossover, and mutation operators to automated prompt engineering [github.com/AmanPriyanshu/GeneticPromptLab, weight 0.49, weak backing]. A thesis adapting GCG-style gradient attacks to the agent setting is further adjacent work [davhofer.github.io, weight 0.44, weak backing].

The program's critique transfers cleanly: a population of discovered attacks scored by an entropy or win statistic is a binary incidence structure (attack × target), and diversity metrics computed on the observed population carry no null. The synthesis document's specific claim that the diversity entropy D satisfies ΔD = 0 on a margin-preserving fibre is a program-computed property, and this corpus records it as such.

## ACUTE: the closest thing to a null, and why it is still short

The ACUTE protocol (ICML 2026, link 5) is the one member of the 22 whose comparison is not purely raw. The synthesis document reports 1000 permutation resamples in its EURO utility-Oracle/Anti-Oracle renormalization, and classifies the null as partial: the resamples are not margin-preserving. The distinction is measurable in standard tooling. scikit-learn's permutation_test_score generates a null distribution by scoring a classifier on 1000 permutations of the dataset labels [scikit-learn.org, weight 0.85], and the classic permutation-test literature for classifier performance establishes the same label-permutation scheme [dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1756006.1859913, weight 0.84]. Label permutation destroys and rebuilds the column structure of a two-way table; it does not preserve both row and column margins simultaneously. The synthesis document's claim is that ACUTE's resampling inherits this limitation, and the classification "partial" rather than "no" reflects the 1000 resamples being genuinely closer to a null than a plain ablation delta.

## Standing caveat

FinanceHarness's leakage barrier and ACUTE's renormalization are real statistical hygiene, and the program does not dispute their value. The gap is the same at both: no between-arm number in either system has been deflected against a null that holds the incidence margins fixed. That measurement is the missing layer this corpus argues for.

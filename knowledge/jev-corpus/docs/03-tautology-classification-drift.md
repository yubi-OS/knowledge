# 03 Tautology Classification and Curve Drift

Scope: how the engine classifies a corpus claim as tautological versus informative, and why measured curves must be re-measured over time because the statistical properties they describe drift.

## Tautology and circular reasoning: related but distinct

Circular reasoning (Latin: circulus in probando, "circle in proving") is a fallacy in which the reasoner begins with what they are trying to end with; notably, it is not a formal fallacy but a pragmatic defect in an argument [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_reasoning) (weight 0.83). Whether a tautology and circular reasoning are the same thing is contested: a tautology is a sentence in which the conclusion is equivalent to its premise, while circular reasoning is a logical fallacy; the two phenomena are related but not identical [2](https://laughterhopesockintheeye.com/2015/01/09/resources-for-a-lesson-plan-on-tautologies-and-circular-reasoning/) (weight 0.06, weak backing). Discussion threads make the same distinction explicitly, noting sources treat them differently [3](https://philosophy.stackexchange.com/questions/34409/difference-between-tautology-and-circular-reasoning) (weight 0.03, weak backing). In applied contexts, the tautology encountered most frequently is the logical fallacy of assuming the conclusion in the premise [4](https://encyclopedia.arabpsychology.com/tautology/) (weight 0.13, weak backing).

The practical stakes of circular claims are visible in the history of science. Evolutionary biology's "survival of the fittest" formulation was criticized as circular; the discipline escaped the trap by turning survival into measurable, testable predictions, transforming circular logic into measurable reality [5](https://www.ecologysci.com/posts/the-tautology-trap-how-evolutionary-biology-escaped-circular-reasoning-and-became-a-predictive-science) (weight 0.43, weak backing). The general lesson, applicable to a documentation corpus: a claim that cannot fail is not evidence, and the fix is to restate it as a measurable assertion. Popular explainers catalog circular reasoning under its alias "begging the question" (petitio principii) and similar names [6](https://practicalpie.com/circular-reasoning/) (weight 0.18, weak backing).

## Concept drift: why re-measurement is mandatory

Concept drift is "an evolution of data that invalidates the data model", occurring when the statistical properties of the target variable change over time [7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_drift) (weight 0.10, weak backing). The working definition in the time-series literature: concept drift refers to unpredictable changes in the statistical properties of target variables over time, which degrade model performance, making accurate drift detection crucial [8](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231225017928) (weight 0.79). Systematic reviews agree that concept drift refers to continuous changes in statistical properties and reduces the effectiveness of forecasting models [9](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11075676) (weight 0.90).

Detection practice gives the concrete machinery. One method family combines Riemannian manifolds with statistical process control, using online segmentations with multiple sliding windows and phase space reconstruction for time series [10](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016003223006361) (weight 0.95). The monitoring playbook is to compare the current distribution to a reference (training data or a recent production window) and quantify divergence with statistical tests; the Population Stability Index is the standard binned comparison [11](https://www.systemoverflow.com/learn/ml-monitoring-observability/concept-drift/detection-strategies-monitoring-drift-with-statistical-signals) (weight 0.09, weak backing). On the response side, evolving fuzzy systems are self-developing, self-learning rule-based systems whose parameters and structure self-adapt online on streaming data [12](http://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Evolving_fuzzy_systems) (weight 0.73).

Together these say: a curve measured once is a snapshot of a moving target. A measurement scheme that never re-measures will silently go stale, and the detection machinery (reference window, divergence statistic, alarm threshold) must be part of the system, not an afterthought [8](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231225017928) (weight 0.79), [10](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016003223006361) (weight 0.95).

## Application in the Jev Corpus engine

The engine's lens lane exposes `tautologyClassify` and `driftPoints`. Classification is deterministic and fixture-anchored: 21 tautology fixtures were generated from the project's actual Python classifier and the TypeScript port must reproduce the exact verdicts, so the contested philosophical boundary is settled by the reference implementation, not re-argued per port. Drift detection consumes the engine's own measured history: each evolution cycle records its measured gains, and drift is computed against the previous cycle's values rather than a frozen baseline, matching the reference-window pattern above. The design deliberately records honest failure ("corpus: {error: ...}") instead of fabricating a drift number when a cycle has no comparable history. (Source: the yubiOS refs source document "Jev Corpus", 2026-10-01.)

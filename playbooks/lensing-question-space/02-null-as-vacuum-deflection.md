# 02: The null is the vacuum, the residual is the deflection

Scope: source doc finding F3: null standardization as deflection measurement against an unlensed background, the tilted-iid random matrix spectrum of the curveball medium, and standardization as a statistical equivalence principle.

Grounding spine: [source doc](file://yubi-OS/yubiOS playbooks/lensing-question-space.md), 2026-08-13, section F3.

## The lensing analogy

The source doc states that null standardization is exactly the operation gravitational lensing calls measuring deflection against the unlensed background (source doc). The dig confirms this is standard practice: in the weak regime most lines of sight produce small distortions measured relative to an unlensed reference ([en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_gravitational_lensing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_gravitational_lensing), weight 0.63, weak), and a 2025 survey chapter derives the key lensing equations as deflection relative to a fiducial geometry ([arxiv.org/abs/2501.07938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07938), weight 0.63, weak). The specific numbers the doc cites, Delta V2 = +0.0144 at z = +12.3 as a deflection angle for the 98%-marginal result, are internal-record claims about the is-this-x paper and are cited here from the source doc alone.

## The medium gets an analytic spectrum

The source doc leans on Lyu and Mukherjee (arXiv:2407.14942) for the claim that fixed-margin random matrices converge to a tilted-iid ensemble with a variance profile, giving the curveball medium an analytic spectrum (source doc). The dig verified the paper exists and says what the doc needs: it studies large random matrices with iid entries conditioned to prescribed row and column sums (margins), connecting the problem to relative entropy minimization, Schrodinger bridges, and contingency tables ([arxiv.org/abs/2407.14942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14942), weight 0.66). The Marchenko-Pastur law, the doc's "MP at constant margins" anchor, is the standard asymptotic spectrum of large rectangular random matrices ([en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marchenko%E2%80%93Pastur_distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marchenko%E2%80%93Pastur_distribution), weight 0.5).

## Statistical equivalence principle

The source doc's closing claim is that standardization is a statistical equivalence principle: locally, coordinates always exist in which the medium is flat, and what survives (Delta V2z, directional Hodge statements, Parseval shares against null) is curvature (source doc). The dig found no direct academic treatment of this exact statement; it is presented as the source doc's framing, backed by the two lensing references above. Weak-backing labels apply to any further extrapolation beyond the source doc.

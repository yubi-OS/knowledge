## Q and N0

Question space Q is the is-this-x triple (Phi, M, V): corpora are rays, families are sources. Null space N0 is the matched-null ensemble, the vacuum metric from which the map originates (source doc). These are internal definitions carried over from the is-this-x program; the dig contributes the physics vocabulary (lens, vacuum) verified in docs 01 and 02.

## I and the Fisher-Rao geometry

The source doc claims intent space I is the sufficient-statistic quotient, and by Cencov's theorem its unique invariant geometry is Fisher-Rao (source doc). The dig backs both halves separately: Fisher-Rao metrics are the standard object of information geometry, with surveys connecting them to phase-transition analysis ([arxiv.org/abs/1512.02077v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/1512.02077v2), weight 0.76) and to metrics induced on probability spaces ([esi.ac.at information geometry pdf](https://www.esi.ac.at/uploads/6074f7c8-3794-4729-9a89-48b8dcf8e20a.pdf), weight 0.53, weak). Cencov's theorem itself surfaced only weakly ([cims.nyu.edu cencov pdf](https://cims.nyu.edu/~gromov/Fall%202026/cencov_theorem_expanded.pdf), weight 0.47, weak): the uniqueness claim stands on the source doc, with this as corroboration only.

## Latent space and the dimension dial

The latent space claim: Zhang's effective dimension D_lambda = tr((T + lambda I)^-1 T) is a continuous dial from 0 (lambda to infinity) to numerical rank (lambda to 0), turning the discrete basis ladder into a dispersion relation where dimension plays wavelength; Johnson-Lindenstrauss gives the floor k = O(epsilon^-2 log n) for how many dimensions a corpus needs (source doc). The dig backs the JL floor: any high-dimensional dataset can be randomly projected to lower dimension while preserving pairwise distances, with k scaling as stated ([scikit-learn.org plot_johnson_lindenstrauss_bound.html](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/miscellaneous/plot_johnson_lindenstrauss_bound.html), weight 0.57, weak). The Zhang effective-dimension formula is internal to the source doc in this dig; no independent citation was found at weight >= 0.5, so it is reported as source-doc claim only.

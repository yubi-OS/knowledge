## F4: over-focus is a caustic

The source doc reports that all 14 machine-precision 1.0000 rows, pure noise included, correspond to rank-2 degeneracy: the ray map degenerating, over-focus that destroys information (source doc). Prop. 2's red flag is therefore a caustic detector, classification belongs to Thom and Arnold catastrophe theory, and the transport analog is Brenier-map singularities (source doc).

The dig backs the classification half: catastrophe optics classifies caustic morphologies and their diffraction patterns, the standard reference being Berry's review ([michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com berry089](https://michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/berry089-1.pdf), weight 0.68, weak), with recent work applying the same classification in charged-particle optics ([sciencedirect.com S0304399125001895](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304399125001895), weight 0.62, weak). Wikipedia's catastrophe theory entry scored 0.46 and is background only. The Brenier-map singularity analog is internal to the source doc and is not independently verified by this dig.
